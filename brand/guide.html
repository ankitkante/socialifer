socialifer

Said so·sha·LIE·fer: "social" + Latin -fer, carrier. The app carries your posts to every platform at once. Write Socialifer in sentences and lowercase socialifer in the wordmark.

Logo: Ring of fire

A glowing ring with sparks streaking out, like a Diwali chakri. The app sends posts out everywhere; the mark sends light out everywhere.

With the name

socialifer

Mark on the left, "socialifer" in Inter Bold with letter-spacing about −2%. The mark is about 1.5× the text height. The web app, the app and socialifer.com all use the plum tile next to live text.

Small sizes

At 32 px and below, use logo-small.svg (fewer, thicker sparks); the full mark blurs.

Colour

Dusk Plum is the one brand colour. Everything else is a neutral with a faint plum tint. Platform logos keep their own colours; green and red are only for ready and problem states.
