Privacy Terms

Write once. Post everywhere.

Socialifer publishes one post, with its photos or videos, to every social platform you use, at the same time.

One page, every platform

Write the post, attach photos or a video, tick the platforms and press Post. Each platform gets its own settings, such as a YouTube title or an Instagram cover.

Runs on your computer

Socialifer runs on your own computer with your own accounts. There's no Socialifer server, so your posts and passwords never pass through us.

Subtitles in Hindi and English

It makes subtitles on your computer, adds them to YouTube as caption tracks, and burns them into the video for the other platforms.

Post from your phone

Write on the Android app, even offline. When the phone is back on your home Wi-Fi, your computer posts it.

Where it posts

YouTube (videos and Shorts), Instagram, X, Threads, LinkedIn, Facebook Pages, Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram and Discord. Socialifer only uses each platform's official API, and only to publish your own posts. It doesn't read your feed or anyone else's content.

Who it's for

Creators who make something once and want it everywhere, without opening each app in turn. Socialifer is a personal project, built by a writer and video creator who posts in Hindi and English, and it isn't open for sign-ups yet.
