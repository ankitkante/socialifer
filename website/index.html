What it does

  1. 01

    One page, every platform

    Write the post, attach photos or a video, tick the platforms and press Post. Each platform shows its own character count and checks as you type.

  2. 02

    Each platform, its own way

    A YouTube title and thumbnail, an Instagram cover or Story, an X thread or poll. Set them once, next to the post.

  3. 03

    Videos that fit

    A video too big for a platform is made smaller for that platform, automatically. Too long is never cut; you decide.

  4. 04

    Subtitles in Hindi and English

    Made on your computer, added to YouTube as caption tracks, and burned into the video everywhere else.

  5. 05

    Post from your phone

    Write on the Android app, even offline. When the phone is back on your home Wi-Fi, your computer posts it.

There's no Socialifer server. Your posts and passwords never pass through us.

It runs on your own computer with your own accounts, and uses each platform's official API only to publish your own posts. It doesn't read your feed or anyone else's content. Privacy policy
