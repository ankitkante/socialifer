Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Socialifer is an app that publishes your posts to several social platforms at once. It runs on your own computer, and its Android app talks only to that computer. There is no Socialifer server: your posts, files and account access are never sent to us. This policy explains what the app does with your data on your devices and the platforms you choose.

What the app handles

  • Account access: the keys or tokens that let the app post to your accounts. They are stored in a settings file on your computer and are only ever sent to the platform they belong to.
  • Your posts: the text, photos, videos, subtitles and settings you choose for each post. They are sent only to the platforms you tick.
  • Results: the link to each published post, and any error a platform returns.

The app does not read your feed, your messages, your followers or other people's content, and it does not use your data for advertising or to train AI models.

Where it's kept, and for how long

  • Photos and videos are copied into the app's folder on your computer while you prepare a post, and deleted automatically after 24 hours. After a video post, a copy of the video is saved to your Videos folder so you can post it by hand where the app can't (such as Substack), and deleted when you press Done.
  • Subtitles are made on your computer. Your video's audio isn't sent to any transcription service. Saved transcripts are deleted 24 hours after they were last used.
  • Posts from the phone are stored on the phone. When it's on your home Wi-Fi, they're sent to your computer over signed requests, kept there until posted and the phone collects the results (7 days at most), then deleted.
  • Activity logs (what was posted where, and any errors) are kept on your computer, and each month's log is deleted once it's over 6 months old.
  • Public links for Instagram and Threads: these platforms fetch photos and videos from a web address instead of taking an upload. While a post is going out, the app makes its files reachable through a temporary tunnel on your computer, at random addresses that can't be guessed.

Google and YouTube

When you connect YouTube, the app asks Google for these permissions, and uses them only as described:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload your video with the title, description, tags, visibility and thumbnail you set.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to check that an uploaded video has finished processing.
  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.force-ssl): to add your subtitle files to the uploaded video as caption tracks.

Google user data is used only to provide these features. It isn't sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models, and it stays on your computer. Socialifer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Socialifer uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Meta, X, LinkedIn and the other platforms

For Instagram and Threads the app asks Meta for basic profile access (to know which account to post as) and permission to publish content. For X, LinkedIn, Facebook Pages, Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram and Discord it asks only for what posting needs. Each platform's own privacy policy covers what it does with your posts once they're published.

Deleting your data

Socialifer holds no copy of your data, so there's nothing for us to delete. To remove everything from your side:

  1. Disconnect the app from each platform: for Google, at your Google Account's third-party connections; for Instagram and Threads, under Apps and websites in their settings; for X, LinkedIn and the others, in their connected apps settings.
  2. On your computer: delete the keys from the app's settings file, or delete the app's folder. This removes its photos, videos, transcripts and logs too.
  3. On your phone: delete posts in the app, or uninstall it.

Posts you published stay on each platform until you delete them there.

This website

socialifer.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It's hosted by Cloudflare, which may process your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. If you email hello@socialifer.com, your message is kept in our inbox and used only to reply.

Children

Socialifer isn't meant for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly handle their data.
